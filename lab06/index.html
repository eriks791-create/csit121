World Wild Life

The website I chose was worldwildlife which can be found at https://www.worldwildlife.org is non profit commited to protecting nature and its resources. I beleive the sites target audience would be people who care about and want to get involved in protecting a perserverving nature and wildlife. I beleive the site is organized in a random or web organization. The CRAP design principle the site seems to use is either contrast or repetition. I don't know what the audit score for the site would be because the accessibility checker has a daily limit that I exeeded. I think the site seems effective in its purpose from what I can tell. the site seems to also be loaded with plenty of useful information for anyone coming to the site. One recommendation I would make is the the the photos need to be updated in some way because the resolution of them seems to be lower quality then intended.
